## Skill shape (X, 2026-07)

Most `SKILL.md` files are too big. Prefer:

- small skill body
- scripts inside the skill
- progressive disclosure

`/eli5 <topic>` → HTML artifact, big pictures, few words, "like you don't know anything about the topic."
