# wayfinder (Matt Pocock)

Foggy work: a shared map of **decision tickets**, not a 50-chapter plan. Plan, don't do (unless Notes override).

## Domain language

- **Map** — one issue labelled `wayfinder:map`; an index, not a store
- **Destination** — what the end looks like; first act of charting
- **Frontier** — open, unblocked, unclaimed children
- **Fog of war** — in-scope but not yet ticket-sharp (`Not yet specified`)
- **Out of scope** — closed, never graduates

## Ticket types

Research (AFK), Prototype (HITL), Grilling (HITL), Task (unblocks a decision, does not deliver the destination).

## Map body

Destination, Notes, Decisions so far (gists + links only), Not yet specified, Out of scope. Refer to tickets by **name**, never a bare id.
